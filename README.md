# Kowloon-VR-Horror-Game
A first-person VR horror game set in Kowloon Walled City.

## About This Project
This is a first-person VR horror game themed around Kowloon Walled City. Guided by spatial narrative theory, the project transforms the labyrinthine urban space into an explorable immersive horror experience, and explores design methods to balance VR immersion and motion sickness tolerance.

## Project Background
Kowloon Walled City carries unique historical and cultural connotations and distinctive spatial characteristics. This project extracts its cultural symbols and spatial logic, translates them into a VR interactive experience, and explores how spatial design can shape narrative rhythm and emotional experience in virtual environments.

## My Role & Contributions
**Role**: Lead Scene & Spatial Narrative Designer

I was responsible for the theoretical design framework, spatial narrative construction, scene construction and VR locomotion mechanism design of the project, and participated in the development of corresponding movement logic.

**Contributions**：
### 1. Three-Tier Design Framework for Spatial Experience
By integrating Threshold Space Theory and Spatial Narratology, established a three-tier design framework consisting of "Cultural Symbols – Spatial Hierarchy – Narrative Rhythm", which serves as the core guiding methodology for the overall scene construction and experience design.

### 2. Scene Construction & Spatial Narrative Design
Integrated the historical and cultural symbols of Kowloon Walled City into the scene layout, and completed the spatial construction of the game environment.
- Restored the representative architectural features and living details of the walled city in the 3D scene to shape the unique historical atmosphere
- Planned the overall spatial layout and exploration route based on spatial hierarchy, building a progressive spatial narrative through first-person roaming
- Arranged environmental trigger nodes along the route to shape emotional rhythm and enhance the immersive horror atmosphere

### 3. Hybrid Locomotion Mechanism Design
Designed a hybrid movement mechanism centered on teleportation combined with limited free movement, balancing immersive experience and motion sickness tolerance.
Participated in the development of corresponding locomotion interaction logic, and completed multiple rounds of user testing and parameter optimization.

## Technical Implementation
- **Engine**: Developed on Unity engine
- **VR Standard**: OpenXR compatible, supports mainstream PC VR headsets
- **Core Mechanics**: First-person VR exploration, environmental trigger system, spatial narrative clue system, hybrid locomotion
